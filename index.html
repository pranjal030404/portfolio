From a device on a truck
to a dot on a map

A GPS tracking platform I built at Atrivis. Trackers in the field talk to it over raw TCP; a browser somewhere else shows where they are. These are the eight things in between.

  1. 01GPS deviceVehicle tracker in the field
  2. 02TCP socketLong-lived connection

    Not HTTP

    Trackers open a socket and hold it. The server reads whatever arrives, whenever it arrives — there is no request to respond to.

  3. 03GT06 / Concox frameBinary payload

    Raw device frame

    Start bits, protocol number, payload, checksum. A bad checksum is cheaper to throw away than a corrupted position is to explain.

  4. 04ParserDecode and validate

    Decode

    Out comes position, speed, heading and status — the first point in the chain where the data means anything.

  5. 05Redis streamQueue

    Decouple ingestion

    Ingest never waits for processing. A slow consumer falls behind and catches up; it doesn't drop packets or take the socket server down.

  6. 06Node.js serviceGeofences, alerts, trips
  7. 07MongoDBPosition history

    Persist

    Route playback, distance and speed analytics all read from here.

  8. 08Live mapReact · Leaflet

    Present

    Anything happening right now goes straight out over Socket.io to the dashboard, and the dot moves.
